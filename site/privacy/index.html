Plain-language policy

Privacy

Effective 28 August 2026

Your files stay local

The web checker reads the file you choose inside your browser. It does not upload the file, preview, filename, camera metadata, or result to us. Closing the tab releases the in-memory result.

No accounts or tracking

RAW Fit Check has no accounts, advertising, analytics, cookies, fingerprinting, or third-party runtime scripts. The CLI makes no network requests.

Ordinary hosting logs

The hosting platform may temporarily process standard request details such as an IP address, timestamp, and requested page to deliver and secure the site. RAW files are never part of those requests.

Offline cache

A service worker caches the public site shell and illustration on your device so the checker remains available offline. It stores no chosen RAW files or reports. You can remove it through your browser's site-data controls.

Questions

Open a privacy issue in the public project repository.
