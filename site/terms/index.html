What the tool provides

RAW Fit Check is a free diagnostic. It reports local container metadata, embedded-preview behavior, and evidence-backed compatibility claims. A result is a preflight, not a guarantee that every edit, export, camera setting, or file from a shoot will work.

Your responsibility

Keep backups and test a small representative set before changing software, deleting originals, or importing a full shoot. Do not treat the benchmark as a recommendation to buy or replace hardware.

Registry evidence

Compatibility information can become outdated as editors and operating systems change. Each built-in claim includes its source and access date. Review the evidence and contribute corrections through the public repository.
